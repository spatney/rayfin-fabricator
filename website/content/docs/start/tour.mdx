Fabricator has two main places: Home, where you choose a project, and the project workbench, where you build, inspect, preview, and publish one app.

## Home

![Home, with the New project, Open folder and Clone from GitHub actions above a list of three recent projects.](/screenshots/home.webp)

Home is titled **Your projects**. Use it to start or open work:

- **New project** creates a Rayfin app and opens it with chat ready, running on this computer.
- **Open folder** opens an existing local project.
- **Clone from GitHub** brings a repository into Fabricator.
- **Recent projects** lists projects you have opened before. A project can show **Open now** or **Missing**.
- **Change folder** changes where new local projects are saved.

If **Team workspaces** is turned on in **Settings → Experiments**, Home also shows the team workspace section. If **Migrate Power BI reports** is turned on, Home also offers **Migrate a Power BI report**. See [Migrate a Power BI report](/docs/build/migrate-power-bi).

## Project app bar

![The Fabricator workbench. On the left, the chat shows a finished Copilot turn that built an expense tracker; on the right, the preview shows the published app with a monthly total and a spending chart.](/screenshots/workbench.webp)

When a project is open, the app bar starts with the project switcher, followed by **Search**. Choose the project name to open a menu of your recent projects, then choose one to switch to it. Choose **All projects** to see every project on Home while the current project keeps running, and choose the `Back to <project name>` button to return to it.

The center of the app bar has project tabs:

- **Build**: chat and the preview.
- **Code**: files, history, skills, and secrets.
- **Blueprint**: how the app is put together: its architecture, data model and the Fabric data it connects to.
- **Advisor**: app health checks and fixes.

The right side has the publish controls:

- The publish control shows the published app's name, or **Not published**, with a dot for its state. Select it for the published app's address, workspace and account, to switch to or add another published app, and to turn **Publish after each chat turn** on or off. See [Publish to Microsoft Fabric](/docs/ship/deploy).
- The button next to it publishes. **Publish** publishes the app for the first time, or again. A label such as **Publish 3 changes** says what's waiting, and **Try again** follows a publish that failed. See [Publish changes](/docs/ship/deploy#publish-changes).
- **Share** shares the published app with people in your organization. It appears once the app is published.

The app bar also has the account menu, set apart from the project's controls. It holds **Settings** too.

## Search

**Search**, at the left of the app bar after the project, finds your projects, the things you can do, the documentation and the open project's files, and asks Help. Select it, or press `Ctrl+K` (`Cmd+K` on macOS) from anywhere in the workbench. See [Search Fabricator](/docs/start/search).

## Build view

**Build** is where you spend most of your time. Chat is on the left. The preview is on the right. During a turn, the chat work log shows what Copilot is doing.

The preview shows your app running on this computer, with its own backend and data, so changes appear as Copilot makes them, and **Output** in the toolbar opens its output. Once that app is published, **Local** and **Published** switch between the two. See [Develop on this computer](/docs/build/local-development).

For a published app that this computer can't run, the preview shows the published app instead. While Copilot works, it shows that app's pages from this computer, at a `localhost` address with the published app's backend, and it goes back to the published app once the turn's changes are published. With **Publish after each chat turn** off, it keeps showing your latest changes from this computer while the app is open.

The preview toolbar includes **Back**, **Forward**, **Reload**, the app's address, **Design**, and **Focus**, plus **Fabric** for an app that works only inside the Fabric portal. It also has **Preview as**, which chooses who the app on this computer signs in as; see [Preview as someone else](/docs/build/preview#preview-as-someone-else). **Design** lets you click your app, queue visual changes, and send them to Copilot. See [Live preview](/docs/build/preview) and [Design mode](/docs/build/design).

## Code, Blueprint, and Advisor

**Code** has four sub-tabs: **Files**, **History**, **Skills**, and **Secrets**. **Files** opens a read-only code viewer. **History** shows a timeline of changes. **Skills** manages reusable Copilot instructions. **Secrets** holds the values your app's functions read, such as API keys. See [Code and history](/docs/build/code-and-history), [Skills](/docs/build/skills) and [Secrets](/docs/build/secrets).

**Blueprint** shows what the app is made of, what it connects to and which identity each connection uses, along with its Rayfin data model and the Fabric data it connects to: semantic models, and the tables of SQL databases, warehouses and lakehouses. Its **Data explorer** shows the data your app stores on this computer. See [Blueprint](/docs/build/data-model).

**Advisor** checks app health. The tab can show a badge for open high or medium issues. See [Check app health with the Advisor](/docs/ship/advisor).

## Account menu and Settings

![The account menu opened from the avatar in the top-right corner, showing the signed-in Fabric account with Manage accounts, Refresh Fabric authentication, Sign out and Settings.](/screenshots/account-menu.webp)

When you are signed in to Fabric, the account menu shows your account initials, and opening it shows your account and its organization. It has **Manage accounts…**, which opens **Accounts** for every account Fabricator uses, plus **Refresh Fabric authentication**, **Sign out** and **Settings**. When you are not signed in, it shows **Sign in to Fabric**, with **Manage accounts…** and **Settings** behind the arrow next to it. See [Manage your accounts](/docs/start/accounts).

Open **Settings** from the account menu. Settings groups its options into **General** (**Accounts**, **API keys**, **Setup**, and **Workspace folder**), **Appearance** (**Theme** and **Text size**), **Diagnostics** (including **Usage stats**), **Experiments** (**Team workspaces** and **Migrate Power BI reports**), and **About** (the versions Fabricator runs on, and links to these docs). See [Settings](/docs/reference/settings).

## Status bar

The footer shows project and app status:

- Git status for local projects, or team workspace and branch for team apps.
- The project's Rayfin version, when a newer Rayfin release is available. See [Keep Rayfin up to date](/docs/ship/rayfin-versions).
- The active Fabric workspace, when known.
- Interface zoom.
- Fabricator app version, such as `v1.7.2`.
- **Help**, which opens the Help assistant. It answers questions about what went wrong, and writes up a bug report when the problem is Fabricator's fault.

## Ray

Ray is Fabricator's stingray. He keeps you company while an app installs its packages: when you create a project, clone one, or open one whose packages are missing. He shares facts about Rayfin and tips for Fabricator, and greets you in Help. The first time Fabricator starts on a computer, he's on the loading screen too, telling you in his own words how it's going. After that, he welcomes you back each time Fabricator opens, and he talks you through any publish you choose to watch.

While Ray is on screen, you can:

- Select him to pet him.
- Drag him somewhere else.
- Select a speech bubble to hear the next fact.
- Point at him and select the **×** next to him to send him away. Until you restart Fabricator, he only appears in Help.

If your computer is set to reduce motion, Ray stays in one place and his speech appears without animation.
